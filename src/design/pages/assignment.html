Assignment

Is the difference real? A short data-analysis report

Introduction to Statistics · STAT 101 · Week 4 → due week 6 · Serves outcome 5 · 2–3 pages · about 4 hours

The point of this assignment is not the arithmetic. It is whether the numbers, the sentence, and the interval in your report agree with each other.

Purpose

You will take a comparison you meet in real life, work out whether the difference is larger than the noise, and say so in plain language a reader can check. This is the skill outcome 5 names: your numbers, your chart, and your sentences agreeing.

Instructions

  1. Choose a comparison with two groups — your own measurements, or a published table. Examples: blue-route versus red-route commute times; two classes' quiz scores; two shops' prices for the same basket.
  2. Describe how the data was collected, in three sentences, including what could bias it.
  3. Summarise each group: a table with the count, the mean, and the spread.
  4. Build a 95% confidence interval for the difference between the two means.
  5. Write one paragraph saying what the interval claims and one thing it does not claim.
  6. Add one chart that supports your sentence, and caption it with what it shows.

Deliverables

  • One document of 2–3 pages: table, chart, interval, paragraph.
  • Your working file, so the arithmetic can be followed.
  • Hand in as one PDF, named lastname-difference.pdf.

Due

Week 6, by the end of Thursday — your instructor confirms the exact date and where to hand it in.
