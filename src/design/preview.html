Ki · design system

The exercise book and the teacher’s red margin

Ki has one visual world and two surfaces. The panel is the narrow frame where Ki talks with the educator; the documents are the artifacts Ki makes. Both are paper: ink for Ki’s voice, red for what needs the educator’s eye, and a margin that carries the review beside the line it belongs to.

Every page in pages/ carries the reference course’s content as demonstration material: Introduction to Statistics, week 4. It is not a real course, and the numbers in it are illustrative.
